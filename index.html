Chapter VI · About

The Chronicler

I build things because I want them to exist. Most of them are local-first: tools that run on your own machine, keep your data there, and do not ask for a subscription.

The Pulse Engine is the open-source community I started around that idea. I also wrote a transformer from scratch, from a character tokenizer to BPE, RoPE and a KV cache, to find out if I understood the internals or only the diagrams.

I work on the business side too: sponsorships, operations and ERP rollouts. Away from the terminal: Soulsborne games, Sanskrit and Buddhist terms, and a Samoyed.
